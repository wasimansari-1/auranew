How to play

Every round the dealer draws one card for Dragon and one for Tiger from a fresh 8-deck shoe. The higher card wins. Aces are low (1), then 2–10, Jack 11, Queen 12, King 13 high. Suits only count for Suited Tie.

Bets are open for 15 seconds. Pick a chip, tap the spots you like, then Place bet (chips still on the table when time runs out are placed for you).

On a tie, Dragon and Tiger bets lose half the stake (the other half is returned). A suited tie is also a tie, so a Tie bet wins on it too.

Bet per spot: €0.10 – €100.00. Your total stake is one bet for the round and the winnings are paid as one win.
