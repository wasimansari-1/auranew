Teen Patti · rules & pays

Two hands are dealt from one fresh 52-card deck every round: Player A and Player B get three cards each, dealt face up one by one (A, B, A, B, A, B). Back the hand you think is stronger while the timer runs.

Pays

Player A wins0.98 : 1
Player B wins0.98 : 1
Tie (equal hands)half of every bet returned

Hands, strongest first

  1. Trail — three of a kind (A-A-A best, 2-2-2 lowest)
  2. Pure sequence — three in a row, same suit
  3. Sequence — three in a row (A-K-Q highest, then A-2-3, then K-Q-J … 4-3-2)
  4. Colour — three of the same suit
  5. Pair — two of the same rank (then the third card decides)
  6. High card — highest card, then the next, then the last

Suits never break a tie. Bet limit per spot: €0.10 – €100. Chips you place are sent together when you tap Place bet (or automatically just before the timer ends). One bet per round; a round you don't bet on you can still watch.

Return to player: 98.95% on either player (exact: tie chance 0.111%).
