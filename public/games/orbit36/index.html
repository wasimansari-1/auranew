Orbit 36 — rules & pays

Every 30 seconds the drum draws 6 balls from 1 to 36, then a 7th, the bonus ball — the same draw for every player. Every ball still in the drum is equally likely.

Tickets: pick exactly 6 numbers (or Quick pick). You can play up to 10 tickets in one draw, each with its own stake. Your tickets stay on the slip for the next draws until you change them.

“5 + bonus” means 5 of your numbers are among the 6 drawn and your 6th number is the bonus ball. Pays include your stake (€1 at 8× returns €8).

First ball: bet whether the first ball drawn is odd or even, low (1–18) or high (19–36). Pays 1.92×.

Timing: bets are open for 15 seconds; press Bet — anything you changed on the slip is also placed for you just before betting closes. Then the 7 balls are drawn (10 seconds) and the result shows for 5 seconds.

Return to player (RTP): tickets 96.45%, first-ball bets 96.00%.

Bet limits for one draw (all tickets and side bets together): €0.10 to €50. Maximum win per draw: €50,000.

Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
