Spiral Derby — rules & pays

Every 30 seconds eight numbered marbles race down the spiral track — the same race for every player. The finishing order is drawn fairly before the start: every order of the eight marbles is equally likely.

Bets are open for 12 seconds. Choose a bet type, set your stake and tap the marbles — each pick goes on your ticket (up to 20 bets). Press Place bets, or your ticket is placed for you just before the gate lifts. Then the race runs for about 13 seconds and the marbles park in the finish rack in their finishing order; the result shows for 4 seconds.
