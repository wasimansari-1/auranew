Trio Towers — rules & pays

Three glass towers each hold ten balls numbered 0 to 9. Every 30 seconds one ball drops from each tower — ball 1, ball 2, ball 3 — and everyone at the table shares the same draw. Bets are open for 18 seconds, the draw takes about 7 seconds, then the winning spots light up and pay.

Pick a chip, tap the spots you like (up to 20 different bets a round), then press Place bets. Chips still on the board are placed for you just before betting closes. Each spot takes from €0.10 to €100.

The sum is ball 1 + ball 2 + ball 3 (0 to 27). Small is 0–13, Big is 14–27. A triple is all three balls the same.

Pays are the total return for a €1 bet, your stake included (×1.92 returns €1.92). RTP is the long-run return of each bet: every bet in Trio Towers returns between 95.6% and 96.0%.

Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
