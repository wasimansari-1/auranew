Jewel Drum — rules & pays

Every 30 seconds the drum stops on one of its 20 jewels — the same draw for every player. Jewels are not equally likely: common jewels pay less, rare ones pay more (chances below).

Bets are open for 18 seconds: pick a chip and tap jewels, a colour (column) or a family (row) — as many as you like; chips on the same spot add up. Press Place bets, or your chips are placed for you just before betting closes. The drum then rolls for about 7 seconds; the result shows until the next round.

Pays include your stake (×10 on €1 returns €10). The NX Emblem counts as Gold and as an Orb. RTP: 95.89% on every jewel, family and Gold; 96.30% on Violet, Emerald and Ruby.

Bet limits for one round (all your spots together): €0.10 to €100. Max win per round: as set by the casino (shown here after your first bet).

Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
