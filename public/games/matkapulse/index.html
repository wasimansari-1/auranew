Matka Pulse — rules & pays

Every 30 seconds two pots draw a number for every player at the same time. The OPEN pot draws three digits, then the CLOSE pot draws three digits. Every digit is drawn from 0–9 with equal chance, and each ball goes back into its pot after it is read, so a digit can come up again.

Each pot's three digits are sorted into its panna (0 counts as the highest digit, so 7, 1, 0 makes 170). The last digit of the panna's sum is its single. Open single and close single together make the jodi. A result reads OPEN – JODI – CLOSE, for example 127 – 09 – 559.

Pays are what you get back for each 1 staked, stake included (€1 on a winning single returns €9.60). Return to player (RTP): 96% on every bet.

Bets are open for 15 seconds. Pick a chip and tap your numbers, as many as you like (up to 20 different bets). Press Place bets, or your chips are placed for you just before betting closes. Then the open pot draws, the close pot draws, and the result shows until the next round starts.

Bet limits for one round (all your bets together): €0.10 to €100.

Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
