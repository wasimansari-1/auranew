Lucky 7 — how to play

One card is dealt from a fresh eight-deck shoe, shuffled for every round. Bets open for 15 seconds, then the host deals the card and settles every bet. Pick one or more spots; your chips on all spots are one bet for the round.

BetWins when the card isPaysRTP

A 7 is neither Low nor High, Odd nor Even: those bets lose when a 7 is dealt. A = 1, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13. Red = ♥ ♦, Black = ♠ ♣.

Chips you place are sent when you tap Place bet, or automatically just before bets close. Rebet repeats your last round; Double doubles the chips on the table.
