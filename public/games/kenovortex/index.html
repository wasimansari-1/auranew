Keno Vortex — rules & pays

Every 30 seconds the machine blows 10 balls out of 40 — the same draw for every player, every number equally likely.

Play: pick 1 to 10 numbers on a ticket (or tap Quick pick), choose your stake and press Bet. Bets are open for 15 seconds; then the 10 balls fly up the tube one by one (10 s) and the result shows for 5 s. On a wide screen you can play two tickets per draw — they go in together.

Pays depend on how many numbers you picked and how many of them are drawn (stake included, e.g. ×45 on €1 returns €45):

Return to player (RTP) per ticket size: 1 pick 96.25% · 2: 96.15% · 3: 95.65% · 4: 96.18% · 5: 96.07% · 6: 96.51% · 7: 96.45% · 8: 96.50% · 9: 95.77% · 10: 96.55%.

Bet limits for one draw (all your tickets together): €0.10 to €100. Maximum win per draw: €50,000.

Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
