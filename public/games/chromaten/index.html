Chroma Ten — rules & pays

Every 30 seconds the drum draws one digit from 0 to 9 — the same draw for every player. Each digit is equally likely. Every digit has a colour: green 1 3 7 9, red 2 4 6 8, and two split digits: 5 is green + violet, 0 is red + violet.

Bets are open for 20 seconds: pick a chip and tap colours, sizes or digits (several chips on a spot add up; you can back as many spots as you like). Press Place bets, or your chips are placed for you just before betting closes. Then the drum rolls and stops on the winning digit (about 6 seconds); the result shows until the next round.

Pays are your total return per spot, your stake included (€1 on Green returns €2 when a green digit is drawn). Each spot is paid on its own and wins are rounded down to the cent. Return to player (RTP): 96% on every bet.

Bet limits for one round (all your spots together): €0.10 to €500. Maximum win per round: €50,000.

Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
