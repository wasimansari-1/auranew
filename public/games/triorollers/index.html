Trio Rollers — rules & pays

Every 30 seconds the three rollers draw one number from 000 to 999 — the same draw for every player. Each roller stops on a digit 0–9; every number is equally likely.

Bets are open for 15 seconds: pick a chip, then tap bets (several chips on a bet add up; up to 20 different bets per round). Press Place bets, or your chips are placed for you just before betting closes. Then the rollers spin and lock one by one, left to right (10 s); the result shows for 5 s.

Pays are the total returned for 1 (a 9.6× win on €1 returns €9.60). Return to player (RTP): 96% on every bet (exact sums 95.95%–96%).

Bet limits for one round (all your bets together): €0.10 to €100. Maximum win per round: €50,000.

Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
