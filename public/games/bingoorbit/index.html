Bingo Orbit · rules & pays

A new draw every 30 seconds, the same for every player. Cards are on sale for 10 seconds: play 1 to 4 cards (5×5, numbers 1–75, free centre), tap Buy — your cards are sent together just before sales close. Then the orbit draws 30 balls, one every half second; your cards are daubed automatically.
