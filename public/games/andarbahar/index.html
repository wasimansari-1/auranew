Andar Bahar — rules & pays

  1. Bets are open for a few seconds each round. Pick a chip and tap Andar or Bahar (you may bet both). Chips left on the table are placed automatically as betting closes.
  2. The dealer turns up the joker card in the middle of the table.
  3. Cards are then dealt one at a time, alternating between the two sides — Andar first, then Bahar, and so on.
  4. The first card with the same rank as the joker wins the round for its side.
