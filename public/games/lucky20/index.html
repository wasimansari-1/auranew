Lucky 20 — rules & pays

Every 30 seconds, 20 different numbers from 1 to 80 light up on the board, one after another. Everyone plays the same draw. Bets are open for 15 seconds; then the 20 numbers are drawn and every bet is settled on the whole draw.

Pick a chip, tap the bets you like (several per round), then press Place bets. Chips still on the console are placed for you just before betting closes. Each bet takes from €0.10 to €100. To bet on the exact first number, tap it on the board.

Pays show what comes back for each €1 staked, your stake included. A sum of exactly 810 loses both Over and Under. Most balls: when two or more colours share the most numbers, every colour bet loses. RTP is the long-run return of each bet.

Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
